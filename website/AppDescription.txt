Speedio is your precision driving dashboard for iPhone and CarPlay — built to keep you aware of your speed, the posted limit, and what’s ahead on the road.

WHAT YOU GET

• Real-time speed display powered by high-accuracy GPS
• Live posted speed limits from multiple map sources, including cached limit data so the app can keep working when your connection is spotty
• Smart overspeed alerts — choose audio, haptic, or both, and set a personal buffer so alerts fire when you actually want them to
• Custom haptics: pick from built-in vibration styles or record your own pattern
• Background alerts: if you’re over the limit while the app is in the background, Speedio can buzz you through a silent notification
• Crash/sudden-stop detection on supported devices, with an instant alert when it matters
• Driving analytics: review each session with a driving score, time-in-limit breakdown, overspeed heat map, and session stats
• Siri and Apple Intelligence: start and end drives, check your speed and limit, ask for your ETA, next-turn distance, distance to destination, and your latest or today’s drive summary — hands-free
• Live Activities and Home Screen widgets so your current speed and limit are visible at a glance
• CarPlay support for driving navigation and alerts
• StandBy mode display when your iPhone is resting on a charger or dock
• Map styles, unit choices (mph / km/h), GPS accuracy modes, voice navigation, and route settings
• Offline speed-limit downloads for supported areas
• Privacy-focused design: location and motion data are used for real-time driving features

Speedio is optimized for Arizona roads with AZ511 camera data and DOT-sourced limit information, and it keeps working wherever GPS and map data are available.
